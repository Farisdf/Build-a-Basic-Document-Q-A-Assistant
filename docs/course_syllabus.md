# CS 201: Data Structures and Algorithms — Course Syllabus

## Course overview

CS 201 is a second-year undergraduate course that covers fundamental data structures
(arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, hash tables, binary search trees, heaps, and graphs)
and the algorithms that operate on them (sorting, searching, traversal, and shortest paths).
The course runs for 13 weeks during the Fall 2025 semester, starting Monday 8 September 2025
and ending Friday 5 December 2025. Reading week is the week of 20 October 2025, and no lectures
or labs take place that week.

## Lectures and labs

Lectures take place twice a week: Monday 9:00 am to 10:30 am and Wednesday 9:00 am to 10:30 am,
in Lecture Theatre B of the Hopper Building. Lab sessions are two hours long and run on
Friday afternoons from 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm in Computer Lab 3. Lab attendance is compulsory,
and students must attend at least 10 of the 12 lab sessions to be eligible to sit the final exam.

## Prerequisites and textbook

Students must have passed CS 101 (Introduction to Programming) with a grade of C or higher.
Familiarity with Python is assumed because all programming assignments are written in Python 3.
The required textbook is "Data Structures and Algorithms in Python" by Goodrich, Tamassia, and
Goldwasser (Wiley, 2013). A copy is on 2-hour reserve at the main library front desk.
