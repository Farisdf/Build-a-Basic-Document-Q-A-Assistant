# CS 201 — Exam Rules and Lab Procedures

## What to bring to exams

Students must bring their university student ID card to every exam, including the midterm.
A student who arrives without a valid ID card will not be admitted to the exam room.
Students may bring pens, pencils, an eraser, and a clear water bottle. Both the midterm and
the final exam are closed-book: no textbooks, notes, or printed material are permitted.

## Calculators and electronic devices

Calculators are not permitted in CS 201 exams because no question requires arithmetic beyond
what can be done by hand. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and any other electronic devices must be
switched off and placed in a bag at the front of the room before the exam begins. Any device
found on a student's person during the exam is treated as an academic integrity breach.

## Arriving late or leaving early

Students who arrive more than 30 minutes after an exam has started will not be admitted.
No student may leave the exam room during the first 30 minutes or the last 15 minutes of the
exam. Students who finish early may leave quietly outside those windows after handing their
paper to an invigilator.

## Lab procedures

Each lab session begins with a 10-minute briefing from the TA, followed by individual work on
the week's lab exercise. Lab exercises are marked in person by a TA during the session:
students raise their hand when finished and the TA checks the code and asks one or two
questions about it. Students must use the lab computers or their own laptop; the code must be
pushed to the student's course GitLab repository before the end of the session so that it
is timestamped.

## Missing a lab

Students who miss a lab because of illness should email the course instructor within
3 days with a medical certificate. An approved absence counts as attended for the purpose
of the 10-of-12 lab attendance requirement, but the lab exercise still receives an incomplete
mark unless it is demonstrated to a TA during office hours within one week.

## Lab computer accounts and software

Lab computers run Ubuntu 24.04 with Python 3.12, VS Code, and Git pre-installed. Students log
in with their university username and password. Files saved to the local disk of a lab machine
are wiped nightly, so all work must be committed and pushed to GitLab or saved to the
student's university OneDrive folder.
